# Returns and Warranty

## Returns
30-day free returns, no questions asked. To start a return, contact support with
your order number; we'll email a prepaid shipping label. Refunds are processed
within 5 business days of receiving the item.

## Warranty
All Canopy products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects in
materials and workmanship. Warranty claims require photo evidence of the defect.
Approved claims are resolved with a replacement or store credit.

## Exceptions
Customised or personalised items are final sale. Items showing damage from field
use (rather than manufacturing defects) are not covered under warranty.
