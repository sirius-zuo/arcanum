# Halcyon Robotics Roadmap 2026

Owner: Dana Okafor, VP of Engineering, with Maren Voss. Internal. This roadmap lists the bets we are making in 2026 and who owns them. Dates are targets, not promises.

## Themes

Three themes guide the year: reliability after the lessons of 2025, higher density in fulfilment centres, and a smoother path from the HX-1 to the HX-2.

## Q1: Reliability foundations

Navigation, led by Tobias Lindqvist, ships Wayfinder 4.4 with a redesigned intersection reservation algorithm and load tests at 80 robots per intersection. Platform, led by Nadia Petrova, finishes the staged rollout tooling so that 5 percent canaries are enforced by the fleet gateway rather than by process. Fleet Operations, led by Priya Raman, adds automated rollback when stalled robot counts exceed a threshold.

## Q2: Dense aisles

Perception, led by Kenji Watanabe, delivers a new pallet detection model that works in poor light and reduces false stops by 30 percent. Navigation introduces one-way zones that can be configured from the console. The target is to run the HX-2 in aisles as narrow as 1.0 metres.

## Q3: HX-2 Pro

We plan to launch the HX-2 Pro, a variant with a 90 kg payload and a second battery slot, in September 2026. It reuses the HX2-BAT-48V pack, so existing customers can share spares. The Pro needs the Wayfinder 5 stack and a new safety lidar, which Perception is validating. A pilot with two customers starts in July. This is a plan and the numbers may change before launch.

## Q4: Platform and ecosystem

Platform opens a public fleet API with webhooks and a software development kit for warehouse management system vendors. Customer Success, led by Samir Haddad, runs an HX-1 to HX-2 migration campaign to retire the old fleet by the end of 2027. Security, led by Elena Sorokina, completes an external audit of the fleet gateway.

## Investments and hiring

We plan to hire 18 people in 2026: six in Navigation, four in Perception, five in Platform and three in Fleet Operations. Finance, led by Rosa Delgado, has set aside budget for a third test hall in Austin.

## Risks

The main risks are battery cell supply for the HX-2 Pro, the length of the Wayfinder 5 validation, and customer appetite for slower, staged software rollouts. We review the roadmap monthly and publish changes in the all-hands notes.

## Not on the roadmap

We are not building a humanoid robot, a drone or a consumer product in 2026. Requests for these go to the Halcyon Lab programme described in the employee handbook.
