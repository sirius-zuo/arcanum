# Vendor Contracts Summary

Owner: Rosa Delgado, Head of Finance and Procurement. Internal. This summary lists Halcyon's main vendor contracts, their terms and renewal dates, so that teams know who to ask before they commit to anything new. Figures are annual unless stated otherwise.

## How we manage vendors

Procurement keeps the contract register and flags every renewal 90 days ahead. Security reviews any vendor that processes company or customer data before signature, as required by the information security policy. Contract owners are named below and are responsible for usage and performance.

## Battery cells: Voltaic Cells GmbH

Voltaic supplies the cells for the HX2-BAT-48V pack. The contract runs until 31 March 2027, with a price of 41 euros per cell and a committed volume of 120,000 cells. Contract owner: Dana Okafor. A single source supply is a known risk, and Procurement is qualifying a second supplier in 2026 for the planned HX-2 Pro.

## Lidar sensors: Aurora Optics

Aurora Optics builds the HLS-360 lidar used in the HX-2 and the older HLS-180 used in the HX-1. The contract runs until 30 June 2026 and renews automatically for one year unless cancelled 90 days earlier. The unit price of the HLS-360 is 310 euros. Contract owner: Kenji Watanabe. Perception is evaluating a successor lidar for the HX-2 Pro.

## Cloud hosting: Nimbus Cloud

Nimbus hosts the fleet gateway, the telemetry pipeline and the production fleet console. The commitment is 1.2 million euros per year under a three year agreement ending 31 December 2027. Contract owner: Nadia Petrova. Data is hosted in the European Union region for European customers and in a United States region for American customers.

## Wireless and 5G: Meridian Networks

Meridian provides private 5G kits that we sell with HX-2 deployments. The contract is renewed every year in January and includes field support for commissioning. Contract owner: Samir Haddad.

## Contract manufacturing: Praxis Assembly

Praxis assembles HX-2 chassis in Poland. The contract covers up to 6,000 robots per year and runs until 31 December 2028. The contract includes a quality clause with a penalty if more than two percent of units fail final inspection. Contract owner: Priya Raman, who also visits the plant every quarter.

## Security and incident tooling

Pager and incident management software costs 38,000 euros per year and is owned by Fleet Operations. Our external security audit firm is engaged until the end of 2026 under a fixed fee agreement, owned by Elena Sorokina.

## Upcoming renewals

2. Meridian Networks, January 2027.
3. Voltaic Cells, 31 March 2027: start renegotiation in autumn 2026.

## Approval rules

Any new vendor above 25,000 euros per year needs approval from Rosa Delgado and the budget owner. Contracts longer than three years need sign-off from Maren Voss.
