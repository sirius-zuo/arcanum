# Halcyon Robotics Employee Handbook

Welcome to Halcyon Robotics. We build autonomous mobile robots that move totes and pallets inside warehouses, and we run a small, practical company. This handbook explains how we work together. Questions about any section go to People Operations at people@halcyon.example.

## Working hours and location

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside those hours you choose when to work. Most staff in the Rotterdam and Austin offices work on site three days per week. Field engineers who support customer warehouses travel up to one week in four and receive a travel allowance described in the expenses section.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays in the country of employment. Unused days up to five carry over into the first quarter of the next year. Sick leave is not capped; tell your manager as early as you can and use the team channel to hand over urgent work. New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave regardless of gender.

## Expenses and equipment

Every new hire receives a laptop, a headset and a 400 euro home office budget. Book travel through the company portal. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day. Submit receipts within 30 days. Purchases above 1,000 euros need approval from your team lead and from Rosa Delgado in Finance before you commit to them.

## Learning and growth

Each person has a yearly learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Engineers are encouraged to spend one Friday per month on the Halcyon Lab programme, where small side projects can become roadmap items. Dana Okafor, our VP of Engineering, reviews Lab proposals every quarter.

## Performance and feedback

We run two review cycles per year, in May and November. Feedback is written, specific and two-way. Your team lead writes the first draft, you add your own reflection, and the two of you meet to agree the final version. Promotions are decided by a committee chaired by Maren Voss, our CEO.

## Code of conduct

We treat each other and our customers with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are never acceptable. If something is wrong, you can talk to your team lead, to People Operations, or use the anonymous ethics line. Reports are investigated within 10 working days and the reporter is never penalised for raising a concern in good faith.

## Safety on the warehouse floor

Robots are heavy and fast. Anyone entering a customer warehouse or our test hall must wear a high-visibility vest and safety shoes, attend the 30 minute site briefing, and carry an emergency stop key. Never reach into a robot's path to retrieve an item. Use the emergency stop and report the event to Fleet Operations, led by Priya Raman. Near misses are logged in the same way as incidents so we can learn from them.

## Security and acceptable use

Company devices are for work and reasonable personal use. All staff follow the Halcyon security policy, owned by Elena Sorokina, our Head of Security. Report lost devices or suspicious messages to security@halcyon.example immediately. Customer data never leaves approved systems.

## Leaving the company

We ask for a notice period of one to three months depending on seniority. Return all equipment on your last day. Access is removed at the end of that day, and your manager arranges a handover of ongoing work and on-call duties before you leave.
