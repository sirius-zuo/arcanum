# HX-1 Autonomous Mobile Robot Datasheet

The HX-1 is the first generation Halcyon warehouse robot and the product that started the company. It is now in maintenance mode. New customers should choose its successor, the HX-2, which has a higher payload and a longer battery runtime. This datasheet remains available for customers with existing HX-1 fleets.

## Overview

The HX-1 moves totes between racking and packing stations in medium sized warehouses. It uses a single front lidar and two cameras. It runs the Wayfinder navigation stack version 2 or version 3 and is supported until the end of 2027. Robots built after 2023 can be upgraded to Wayfinder 4 for a fee, but the older compute board limits the number of robots per intersection.

## Battery and charging

The HX1-BAT-36V pack gives a runtime of 5 hours in typical duty. The robot docks itself when the pack falls below 25 percent. Charging from 20 to 80 percent takes 90 minutes, so most customers swap batteries by hand at shift change. The pack is rated for 1,200 full cycles. Packs are no longer manufactured after 2026, and the HX-2 pack is not compatible.

## Safety

The HX-1 has a safety lidar and an emergency stop button on the rear. The protective field slows the robot when a person comes within two metres and stops it at 80 centimetres. Customers who upgrade to the HX-2 benefit from the improved four camera coverage.

## Migration to the HX-2

Halcyon runs a trade-in programme for HX-1 robots. A customer who orders HX-2 units receives credit for each returned HX-1, and Customer Success, led by Samir Haddad, plans the migration with the customer in stages so that both generations can share the same floor. Mixed fleets run through one fleet gateway.

## Support and service

The standard warranty is 12 months, with extensions available until the end of support. Spare HX1-BAT-36V packs and HLS-180 lidars are available while stock lasts. Contact Customer Success for service requests.
