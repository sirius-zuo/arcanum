# HX-2 Autonomous Mobile Robot Datasheet

The HX-2 is the second generation Halcyon warehouse robot, designed for tote and pallet transport in dense fulfilment centres. It replaces the HX-1 and runs the Wayfinder navigation stack version 4 or later. The HX-2 is available from the first quarter of 2025.

## Overview

The HX-2 carries goods on a lifting platform between racking, packing stations and dock doors. It navigates with a lidar and four depth cameras produced by the Perception pipeline, and it coordinates with other robots through the fleet gateway. The chassis is made of aluminium and recycled composite, and the drive wheels are replaceable in under ten minutes.

## Battery and charging

The HX2-BAT-48V pack gives a runtime of 8 hours in typical mixed duty with a full payload. The robot returns to a charging dock on its own when the pack falls below 20 percent. Fast charging from 20 to 80 percent takes 45 minutes, and opportunity charging during breaks is recommended. Packs are rated for 2,000 full cycles before capacity drops under 80 percent.

## Safety

The HX-2 has a certified safety lidar, an emergency stop button on each corner and a protective field that reduces speed to 0.5 m/s when a person is within three metres. The robot stops completely at one metre. Compliance details for each region are listed in the customer documentation.

## Fleet and software

Robots join a fleet through the fleet gateway and receive firmware updates through staged rollouts. A single gateway supports up to 400 HX-2 robots. Telemetry is encrypted in transit, and the production fleet console requires multi-factor authentication.

## Options

Optional accessories include a tote lifting kit, a pallet fork adapter, a barcode reader and a heated battery kit for cold storage down to minus 10 degrees Celsius. The heated battery kit reduces runtime by about one hour.
