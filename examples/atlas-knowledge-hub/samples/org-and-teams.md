# Halcyon Robotics: Organization and Teams

This page describes how Halcyon Robotics is organised, who leads each team and what each team owns. It is maintained by People Operations and reviewed every quarter. Current as of January 2026.

## Executive team

Maren Voss is the founder and CEO. Four leaders report to her: Dana Okafor (VP of Engineering), Priya Raman (Head of Fleet Operations), Samir Haddad (Head of Customer Success) and Rosa Delgado (Head of Finance and Procurement). Elena Sorokina, Head of Security, reports to Dana Okafor and has a dotted line to Maren Voss for audit topics.

## Engineering

Engineering has about 60 people in three teams, all reporting to Dana Okafor.

### Navigation team

The Navigation team owns the Wayfinder navigation stack: route planning, obstacle avoidance and the traffic manager that keeps hundreds of HX-2 robots from blocking each other in narrow aisles. The team is led by Tobias Lindqvist and has 14 engineers split between Rotterdam and Austin. Wayfinder runs on every HX-1 and HX-2 robot in the field.

### Perception team

The Perception team owns the camera and lidar pipeline, the pallet detection models and the calibration tools used at commissioning. It is led by Kenji Watanabe. Perception works closely with Navigation, because Wayfinder consumes the obstacle maps that Perception produces.

### Platform team

The Platform team owns the fleet gateway, the cloud services, the telemetry pipeline and the production fleet console. Its lead is Nadia Petrova. Platform also runs the build and release tooling for robot firmware.

## Fleet Operations

Fleet Operations, led by Priya Raman, runs the robots we operate on behalf of customers and supports customers who run their own fleets. The team maintains the fleet operations runbook, runs the on-call rotation and owns incident response. Each engineering team provides one on-call lead per rotation; the current names are kept in the runbook, not on this page, because they change every few weeks.

## Customer Success

Customer Success, led by Samir Haddad, handles onboarding, training and renewals. It keeps the customer FAQ and works with Fleet Operations when a customer reports a robot problem.

## Security and Finance

Security, led by Elena Sorokina, owns the information security policy and runs audits. Finance and Procurement, led by Rosa Delgado, manages budgets and the vendor contracts summary.
