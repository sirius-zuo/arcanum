# Halcyon Robotics Customer FAQ

This document answers the questions customers and prospects ask most often. It is maintained by Customer Success, led by Samir Haddad. If your question is not covered, contact support at support@halcyon.example.

## Getting started

### How long does it take to deploy a fleet?

A typical deployment takes six to ten weeks from contract to first live shift. The first two weeks cover a site survey, the next three cover mapping and commissioning with our field engineers, and the final weeks are used for a supervised pilot on a small zone before the whole warehouse goes live.

### Do I need to change my warehouse layout?

Usually not. The robots navigate between existing racking. We ask for aisles of at least 1.2 metres for the HX-2 and mark the floor at charging docks. If your aisles are narrower, the site survey will propose one-way zones.

### Which robot should I choose?

New deployments should choose the HX-2, which carries more and runs longer than the older HX-1. HX-1 fleets remain supported until the end of 2027, and we offer a trade-in programme for customers who want to migrate.

## Operations

### What happens if the Wi-Fi goes down?

Robots keep working on their current task and finish it safely, then wait at a safe spot. They do not receive new missions until connectivity returns. We recommend a second access point in every large zone.

### What happens if a robot breaks down?

The robot stops and reports the fault. Fleet Operations contacts your shift lead, and a field engineer is dispatched according to your service plan. Because the production fleet console can command robots, only staff with multi-factor authentication can use it.

### Why do software updates take several days now?

After a serious outage in March 2025 we changed how updates are delivered. Every release now goes to 5 percent of your robots first and waits at least 24 hours before reaching the rest of the fleet. This staged rollout makes updates slower but much safer.

## Data and privacy

### Who owns the data collected by the robots?

You do. Halcyon processes telemetry and warehouse maps only to run and improve the service. Customer layouts are classified as confidential and are stored in approved systems. You can ask for deletion at any time through Customer Success.

### Can I connect the robots to my warehouse management system?

Yes. The fleet gateway offers a REST interface and an event stream. Most customers connect their warehouse management system in the second week of commissioning. Our integration guide describes the mission and status messages.

## Contracts and support

### What is covered by the warranty?

The HX-2 includes 24 months of warranty and the HX-1 includes 12 months. Wear parts such as drive wheels are covered for the first year. Extended plans are available.

### How do I escalate a problem?

Use the support line first. If a fleet is down, tell the agent so that the ticket is raised to a page for Fleet Operations, led by Priya Raman. Renewal and commercial questions go to your account manager.

## Sustainability

All HX-2 packs are collected for recycling at end of life, and the chassis uses recycled composite. Ask Customer Success for the annual sustainability report.
