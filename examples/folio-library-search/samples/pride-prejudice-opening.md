# Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen
## Chapter 1

It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good
fortune, must be in want of a wife. Mrs. Bennet announced to her husband that
Netherfield Park had at last been let, to a wealthy young man named Mr. Bingley.

## Chapter 3: The Meryton Ball

At the ball, Mr. Bingley danced happily with Jane Bennet. His friend Mr. Darcy,
however, stood aloof. When urged to dance with Elizabeth Bennet, Darcy said within
her hearing, "She is tolerable, but not handsome enough to tempt me." Elizabeth
told the story with great spirit among her friends.
