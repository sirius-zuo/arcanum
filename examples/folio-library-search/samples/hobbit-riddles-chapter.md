# The Hobbit — J.R.R. Tolkien
## Chapter 5: Riddles in the Dark

Deep under the Misty Mountains, Bilbo Baggins found a cold ring of gold lying on
the floor of the tunnel and put it in his pocket. There he met Gollum, a slimy
creature who proposed a riddle contest: if Bilbo won, Gollum would show him the
way out; if Bilbo lost, Gollum would eat him.

They traded riddles in the dark. At last Bilbo, flustered, asked aloud, "What
have I got in my pocket?" Gollum took it for a riddle and failed to answer.
Enraged when he discovered his ring was lost, Gollum gave chase, and Bilbo—
unseen, wearing the ring—escaped, following Gollum unknowingly to the exit.
