# Anna Karenina — Leo Tolstoy
## Chapter 1

Happy families are all alike; every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.
Everything was in confusion in the Oblonskys' house. The wife had discovered that
the husband, Stiva, had been carrying on an affair with their former governess.

## Chapter 2

Dolly, the wife, would not leave her rooms. Stiva, good-natured and weak, felt
sorry not for what he had done but that he had not managed to hide it better. The
household—children, servants, relations—all felt the disorder of the family's
broken peace.
