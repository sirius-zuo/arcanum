# The Left Hand of Darkness — Ursula K. Le Guin
## Series: Hainish Cycle
## Chapter 1: A Parade in Erhenrang

I'll make my report as if I told a story, for I was taught as a child on my
homeworld that Truth is a matter of the imagination. Genly Ai, the Envoy of the
Ekumen, walked in the procession through the streets of Erhenrang, capital of
Karhide, on the world its people call Gethen and outsiders call Winter.

The cold was constant, the politics labyrinthine. Ai had come to persuade Gethen
to join the Ekumen, but the strangeness of its people—and his own slowness to
understand them—would shape everything that followed.
