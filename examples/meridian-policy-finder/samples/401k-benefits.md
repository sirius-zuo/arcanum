# 401(k) Retirement Benefits

## Company Match

The company matches 100% of the first 3% of salary you contribute, plus 50% of
the next 2%. The maximum effective match is 4% of your salary.

## Vesting

Company match contributions vest on a 3-year cliff schedule: you become 100%
vested after three years of service. Your own contributions are always 100%
vested.

## Fund Options

- Target-date retirement funds
- Low-cost index funds (S&P 500, total bond market)
- ESG (environmental, social, governance) fund

## Enrollment

Eligible after 90 days of employment. A Roth 401(k) option is available in
addition to the traditional pre-tax contribution.
