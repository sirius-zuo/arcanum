# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility and Duration

- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 4 weeks of fully paid leave.

Applies equally to birth, adoption, and foster placement. Employees are eligible
after 12 months of continuous employment.

## Interaction with PTO

Parental leave is separate from PTO and is not deducted from your PTO balance.

## Interaction with Performance Reviews

If your leave overlaps the Q4 performance review cycle, your review is postponed
to the following quarter. Your eligibility for a merit increase is preserved and
applied retroactively.

## Return to Work

Notify your manager and HR at least four weeks before your planned return. A
phased return-to-work schedule is available on request.
