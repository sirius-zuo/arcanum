# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual Rates

| Tenure | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| 0–1 year | 10 days |
| 1–3 years | 15 days |
| 3+ years | 20 days |

Part-time employees accrue PTO pro-rated to their scheduled hours. An employee
working 50% of full-time hours accrues 50% of the rate above.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year. Days beyond
5 are forfeited on December 31.

## Requesting PTO

Submit requests through the HR portal. Requests of 3 or more consecutive days
require manager approval and at least two weeks notice. Sick leave is tracked
separately and does not count against your PTO balance.
