# Performance Review Process

## Annual Cycle

| Phase | Timing |
|---|---|
| Self-review | October |
| Manager review | November |
| Calibration | Early December |
| Ratings delivered | December 15 |

## Rating Scale

A five-point scale: 1 (Does Not Meet), 2 (Partially Meets), 3 (Meets),
4 (Exceeds), 5 (Significantly Exceeds). Merit increases are tied to the final
calibrated rating.

## Employees on Leave

If you are on parental or extended leave during the review cycle, your review is
postponed to the quarter following your return. Merit increases are applied
retroactively to preserve fairness.

## Promotions

Promotion nominations are submitted by managers during calibration and reviewed
by the department head before ratings are finalized.
