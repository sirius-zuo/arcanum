# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual NDA is entered into between Company A and Company B (the "Parties").

## Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public technical or business information
disclosed by one Party to the other.

## Obligations
Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other's Confidential
Information for a term of three (3) years from the date of disclosure.

## Permitted Disclosures
A Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided it gives
prompt written notice, or with the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

## Survival
The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement survive termination for a period
of five (5) years.

## Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
