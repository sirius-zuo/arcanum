# Data Processing Addendum (GDPR)

This DPA supplements the SaaS Vendor Agreement between TechCorp Inc. (Processor)
and Vantage Capital Partners (Controller).

## Roles
Controller determines the purposes and means of processing; Processor processes
personal data only on documented instructions.

## Data Residency
EU personal data shall be processed within the European Economic Area (EEA) only,
in accordance with GDPR Article 46 transfer safeguards.

## Sub-processors
Processor maintains a list of approved sub-processors and will notify Controller of
any intended changes, giving Controller the opportunity to object.

## Data Subject Rights
Processor assists Controller in responding to data subject requests within
seventy-two (72) hours.

## Breach Notification
Processor notifies Controller of any personal data breach within twenty-four (24)
hours of becoming aware.

## Return and Deletion
On termination, Processor returns or deletes all personal data at Controller's
election.
