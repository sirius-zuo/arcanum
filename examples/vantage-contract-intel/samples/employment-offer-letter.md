# Executive Employment Agreement

This Agreement is between Vantage Capital Partners ("Employer") and Jane Smith
("Executive"), Senior Managing Director.

## Compensation
Base salary plus carried interest participation as set out in Schedule A.

## Intellectual Property Assignment
All work product created by Executive during employment is the sole property of
Employer. Executive assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product
to Employer.

## Non-Compete
For twelve (12) months following termination, Executive shall not engage in
competing investment-management activities within the specified geography.

## Non-Solicitation
For twenty-four (24) months following termination, Executive shall not solicit
Employer's clients or employees.

## Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
