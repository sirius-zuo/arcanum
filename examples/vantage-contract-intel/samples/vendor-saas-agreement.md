# SaaS Vendor Agreement

This Agreement is between TechCorp Inc. ("Vendor") and Vantage Capital Partners
("Client").

## Services and SLA
Vendor provides the hosted platform with a 99.9% uptime service level commitment.

## Payment Terms
Fees are billed annually, net-30.

## Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify Client against third-party intellectual-property claims
arising from the platform. The indemnification cap is two times (2×) the annual
subscription fees.

## Data Ownership
Client retains all rights to Client data. Vendor receives no ownership interest.

## Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days
written notice.

## Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
