# CRISPR-Cas9 Delivery in Primary Neuronal Cultures

## Summary

We compared lipid nanoparticle (LNP) and viral delivery of CRISPR-Cas9 in primary
neuronal cultures. LNP formulations achieved 67% editing efficiency at the target
locus with minimal off-target effects, while maintaining 94% cell viability.

## Discussion

Blood-brain barrier penetration remains the principal challenge for in vivo
applicability. LNP surface modification with brain-targeting ligands is a
promising avenue. The delivery mechanism is independent of the EGFR and JAK2
pathways studied elsewhere in this corpus.
