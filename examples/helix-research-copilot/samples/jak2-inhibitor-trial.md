# Phase 2 Trial: JAK2 Inhibitor Program — Safety and Efficacy

## Cohort Structure

Dose escalation across three cohorts: 100 mg, 200 mg, and 400 mg once daily.

## Adverse Events

A dose-limiting toxicity (Grade 3 elevated liver enzymes) was observed at 400 mg
in cohort 3. The maximum tolerated dose (MTD) was established at 300 mg. The
overall adverse event profile: fatigue (42%), nausea (28%), and ALT elevation
(18%).

## Efficacy

The objective response rate (ORR) was 34% at the MTD. Progression-free survival
data are maturing. The JAK2 inhibitor showed selectivity over EGFR in off-target
panels.
