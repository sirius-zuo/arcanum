# Structure–Activity Relationships of Novel EGFR Kinase Inhibitors

## Abstract

We characterise a series of small-molecule EGFR inhibitors. Compound 17g
demonstrated potent inhibition of EGFR with an IC50 of 2.3 nM in a kinase assay,
while Compound 22b showed weaker activity (IC50 = 14.7 nM). EGFR overexpression
correlates with poor prognosis in non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) cohorts.

## Methods

Binding was measured by surface plasmon resonance (SPR). Downstream pathway
inhibition was confirmed by Western blot for phospho-EGFR and phospho-ERK.

## Results

The 4-anilinoquinazoline core was essential for potency. Substitution at the
6-position improved selectivity for EGFR over related kinases including JAK2.
